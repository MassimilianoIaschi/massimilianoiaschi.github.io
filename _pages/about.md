    My first real passions were <strong>poetry</strong> and <strong>art</strong>. During my first year of high school I used to spend hours inside of art museums everyday during summer, sometimes even just for looking at a couple of paintings.
  </p>

  <p>
    Studying art allowed me to discover <strong>Leonardo da Vinci</strong>. Studying Leonardo allowed me to discover <strong>robotics</strong>. Studying robotics allowed me to discover <strong>my future</strong>.
  </p>

  <p>
    I'm a senior undergraduate student at <strong>Georgia Institute of Technology</strong> in <strong>Mechanical Engineering</strong>, minoring in both <strong>robotics</strong> and <strong>bio-inspired design</strong>, as well as an undergraduate researcher at <strong>Daniel I. Goldman's CRAB Lab</strong>. My research interest is <strong>Bio-inspired Robotics</strong>. Currently I am mainly working on <strong>complex-terrain multilegged robotic locomotion</strong>, but in general I am interested in <strong>exploratory/search-and-rescue robotics</strong>.
  </p>

  <p>
    In summer 2023 I was a research intern at <strong>Roland Siegwart's Autonomous Systems Lab</strong> at <strong>ETH Zurich</strong>, working on the underwater robotics startup <strong>Tethys Robotics</strong>.
    In summer 2022 I was a research intern at <strong>Domenico Prattichizzo's SIRSLab</strong> at <strong>Siena University</strong>, working on <strong>robotic haptic applications</strong>.
  </p>
